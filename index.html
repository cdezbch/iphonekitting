How this works

Every iPhone on this order is already registered to Boston Children's Hospital in Apple Business Manager (ABM). The first time a phone is powered on and connected to Wi-Fi, it automatically downloads BCH's management profile (MDM) and then installs BCH's apps on its own. Your job is to label each phone, give it cellular service, walk it through setup, set a few settings, open two apps once, check it, and pack it.

  1. PrepareSupplies, Wi-Fi, charge
  2. LabelSerial label on the phone's back
  3. SIM / eSIMCellular service + record numbers
  4. Setup AssistantPower on, enroll in BCH management
  5. Hubb appOpen and allow permissions
  6. Apps installedWait until every app finishes
  7. SettingsAutoFill, Live Activities, 5G, App Store
  8. Rover & HaikuOpen once, allow prompts
  9. Quality checkVerify and log each phone

The rules that matter most

If you remember nothing else, remember these. Breaking one usually means the phone has to be erased and set up again.

  1. Never sign in with an Apple Account (Apple ID) — not during setup, not in the App Store, not in Settings.
  2. Never skip Remote Management. If the Remote Management screen does not appear during setup, stop working on that phone and follow Troubleshooting.
  3. Never sign in to any app (Hubb, Rover, Haiku, Locker, or any other) with any username or password. Only accept or allow the prompts described here.
  4. Keep phones on Wi-Fi and on power until every app has finished installing.
  5. Do not install, delete or move apps, and do not change settings that aren't in this guide.
  6. When something doesn't match this guide, set the phone aside and ask BCH — don't improvise.

1Label the phone

The serial number label goes on the back of the iPhone itself — not on the case.

  1. Print the label. It must show:
    • The iPhone serial number as a Code 128 barcode TBD: confirm barcode type and as readable text
    • TBD: anything else? (asset tag, "Property of Boston Children's Hospital", phone number, IMEI)
    Label size: TBD. The serial number is printed on the iPhone box.
  2. Wipe the back of the phone with an alcohol wipe and let it dry.
  3. Apply the label: TBD: exact spot — suggested: lower third of the back, centered, horizontal.
    • Do not cover the cameras, flash, or microphone.
    • Keep it flat, straight, with no bubbles or lifted corners. Press firmly for a few seconds.
  4. Make sure you labeled the right phone: after setup, the serial in Settings›General›About must match the label (checked again in Quality check).

2SIM / eSIM

Read first

iPhone 14 and newer models sold in the US have no physical SIM tray — they are eSIM only. Use Option A or B below based on what BCH confirms: TBD: physical SIM or eSIM? Carrier?

Option A — Physical SIM card

  1. With the phone powered off, eject the SIM tray with the eject tool.
  2. Insert the SIM card assigned to this phone: TBD: any SIM in any phone, or a specific SIM per serial?
  3. Close the tray fully.
  4. Record the SIM's ICCID (printed on the SIM card carrier) against the phone's serial in the manifest.

Option B — eSIM

  1. Activation method: TBD: carrier pre-assigns eSIM to each IMEI/EID (activates on its own during setup) OR a QR code per phone
  2. During setup, if a Cellular Setup or Carrier eSIM Available screen appears, tap Continue / Activate. If using QR codes, scan the code assigned to this phone.
  3. Record the phone's EID and IMEI (Settings›General›About) in the manifest.

In both cases, record the phone number once service is active (Settings›Cellular → My Number) — checked in Quality check.

4Hubb app

Wait until the Hubb icon finishes installing (no gray icon or progress ring), then:

  1. Tap Hubb to open it. TBD: does Hubb ask for a login, site code or QR code? If so, what should CDW do?
  2. Data & Privacy: tap Accept.
  3. Location: iOS first offers only Allow Once / Allow While Using App / Don't Allow. Tap Allow While Using App. When a second prompt offers Change to Always Allow, tap it.
  4. For every other pop-up (Notifications, Bluetooth, Motion & Fitness, Local Network, etc.) tap Allow / Yes / Always Allow.
  5. Go through the remaining in-app screens and tap I Understand on each.
  6. Double-check location: Settings›Apps›Hubb›Location must be Always, and Precise Location must be on. Fix it here if the in-app prompt was missed. Hubb location = Always, Precise Location on.

6Device settings

Shortcut: the CMDI GCT app

The CMDI GCT app (installed with the other BCH apps) walks you through these settings screen by screen. Open it and follow its prompts, then use the list below to confirm nothing was missed. TBD: confirm GCT installs automatically; which steps it covers; add a screenshot

6.1  AutoFill — use Locker

  1. Go to Settings›General›AutoFill & Passwords.
  2. Turn on AutoFill Passwords and Passkeys.
  3. Under AutoFill From, turn Locker on. TBD: should Passwords (Apple / iCloud Keychain) be turned OFF so Locker is the only option? Locker shows a checkmark / is toggled on.

6.2  Locker — Live Activities

  1. Go to Settings›Apps›Locker›Live Activities.
  2. Turn Live Activities on. If More Frequent Updates appears, turn it on too. TBD: confirm both should be ON Live Activities toggle is green.

6.3  Cellular — 5G data mode

  1. Go to Settings›Cellular›Cellular Data Options›Data Mode.
  2. Select Allow More Data on 5G. Checkmark next to "Allow More Data on 5G". This option only appears once cellular service is active. If it's missing, check the SIM/eSIM (Troubleshooting).

6.4  App Store — automatic downloads

  1. Go to Settings›Apps›App Store.
  2. Under Automatic Downloads: turn App Downloads on and App Updates on.
  3. Under Cellular Data: turn Automatic Downloads on, then tap App Downloads and choose Always Allow. Automatic Downloads on; Cellular → App Downloads = Always Allow.

7Rover & Haiku — first launch

Rover

  2. Tap Accept / Allow on every prompt (notifications, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, local network, etc.).
  3. Stop at the sign-in screen. Swipe up to close the app.

Haiku

  1. Open Haiku.
  2. Tap Accept / Allow on every prompt.
  3. Stop at the sign-in screen. Swipe up to close the app.

BTroubleshooting & when to stop

Stop and set the phone aside when…
  • The Remote Management screen never appeared, or someone signed in with an Apple Account.
  • The phone is damaged, won't power on, or has a serial not on BCH's order list.
  • The same problem repeats after following the fix below once.

Record the serial number and the problem in the manifest Notes column and report it to BCH. Do not ship it.

ProblemWhat to do
No Remote Management screen — setup went straight to the Home ScreenThe phone did not reach BCH's management. Erase it: Settings›General›Transfer or Reset iPhone›Erase All Content and Settings, then restart setup on a Wi-Fi network that meets the requirements. If it happens again, stop and report the serial — it may not be assigned in ABM.
"Unable to connect to the configuration server" or enrollment errorCheck the Wi-Fi has internet and no sign-in page. Tap Try Again. If it fails twice, power off and report.
Apps stuck on Waiting… / Loading…Confirm Wi-Fi is connected. Restart the phone (hold side + volume button, slide to power off, power on). Wait 15 more minutes. Still stuck: report the serial and app names.
An expected app never appearsWait 30 minutes on Wi-Fi, restart once. Still missing: report — don't install it from the App Store.
No service / "No SIM" / SOS onlyPhysical SIM: power off, reseat the SIM. eSIM: check Settings›Cellular for a pending eSIM to activate. Still no service: record ICCID/EID and report.
"Allow More Data on 5G" is missingCellular service isn't active yet, or no 5G coverage at your site. Note it in the manifest; BCH will decide whether to ship.
Hubb location isn't "Always"Settings›Apps›Hubb›Location → Always; turn on Precise Location.
Accidentally tapped "Don't Allow" in an appOpen Settings›Apps›(app name) and turn the permission back on.
Signed in with an Apple Account by mistakeStop. Erase the phone (see first row) and set it up again from Step 3. Tell BCH.
